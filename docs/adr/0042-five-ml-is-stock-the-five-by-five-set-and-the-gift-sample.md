# 5 ml is a stocked volume never sold alone: it fills the «Discovery Set 5×5 мл», a second Discovery set row, and a first order's gift sample, a free line that is no discount

Sardor (2026-11-18, ticket #79): 5 ml is not sold as a single bottle. It exists inside the new **Discovery Set 5×5 мл** (99 000, from the customer's calculator) and as the **gift sample** of first customers. The calculator also offers the after-set discount on any 30 / 50 ml, where the TZ says a fragrance of the set; the scope becomes a setting.

**The 5 ml volume.** `volumes.ml` allows 5 (`volumes_ml_check`: 5, 10, 30, 50). Every fragrance has one 5 ml row: the migration adds it to each fragrance, and the admin form and the Excel import add it to each new one (`createWithin`). It has no price (0) and never shows in the form, the workbook or the catalog's cost block. Its size is `NOT_SOLD_ALONE_ML` (5) in the contracts: the name says the rule, since 5 ml is also the 5 × 5 ml set's vial. It is stock like any volume: the stock page lists it, a bottling fills it (ADR 0041, `liquidNeeded` of 5 ml), the warehouse workbook has a 5 ml column, the stock value counts it at its unit cost (no box, no card, ADR 0040). **It is never sold alone**: every shop read of `volumes` adds `sellableVolume()` (the catalog list and card, the cart's add and read, «Купить сейчас», the profitability page), and the cart and buy-now bodies take 10 / 30 / 50 only. A cart row naming a 5 ml (it cannot be added) reads as gone. The stock page lists it, for managers too (they count samples and bottle them), under its fragrance with the other volumes. Its «Нет в наличии» and «Мало» filters and the admin Home's stock queue leave it out: they are about what the shop sells, and otherwise every fragrance would show there until its samples are bottled.

**The second Discovery set.** `discovery_set` is keyed by the ml of its vials (`vial_ml` 10 or 5, the old boolean id is gone) and `discovery_set_items` by `(vial_ml, position)`, so each set has its own price, photo, Uzum link, composition and on hand. The migration adds the 5 × 5 ml row at 99 000 (the calculator's price) flagged `is_placeholder` until the owner saves it, with the 5 × 10 ml set's five as its first composition; the catalog seed writes its 10 boxes only onto a row still flagged, so ours never reach launch unmarked. A Discovery line is told apart by its `ml`, which is the set's vial size (an order line already had `ml`; a cart line gets `vial_ml`). The reserve, the handover's on-hand shelf (keyed by vial ml) and the cost snapshot (`setCost` at the line's `ml`) read it. The builder set keeps the 5 × 10 ml price, a gift promo code's set is the 5 × 10 ml one, and so are the favourite heart, the share link, the reviews block and the profitability page's set: the 5 × 5 ml set has none of those yet. Checkout locks the set rows the lines hold in ascending vial ml, before any volume; the handover does the same. The routes take `?vialMl=5` (`GET /sets/discovery`, the cart's `POST /cart/sets/discovery` body, the admin `GET/PUT /admin/sets/discovery`, `/on-hand`, `/photo`); without it they mean the 5 × 10 ml set, so nothing that existed changes. «Купить сейчас» reads `discovery_set:5`. What tells the two sets apart lives in one descriptor, `DISCOVERY_SETS` keyed by `DiscoveryVialMl` (the stock row's and audit target's key `discovery` / `discovery-5`, the Mini App route, the buy-now text, whether it has the heart, share link and reviews yet), and their texts in i18n maps keyed the same way (`sets.discovery[vialMl]`, the admin's `sets.priceHint[vialMl]`); callers look up, never switch. Audit entries carry `vialMl` in their payload, and the audit page names the set («Discovery set 5 × 5 мл»), never its key.

**A set bottling.** «Bottled from liquid like the vials» (the ticket): `POST /admin/warehouse/sets/:vialMl/bottlings {quantity}` fills boxes straight from the oil of the set's five, one vial of its size from each per box, by `liquidNeeded` with the filling loss. In one transaction: the set row first (as checkout takes it), then the five fragrances in ascending id (`FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, the warehouse's lock); each fragrance's oil falls with a `bottling` stock move (no bottles, `details.set`) and a `bottlings` row marked with the set (`set_vial_ml`; its `lines` are then the set's vials, never bottles on hand), and the set's on hand rises. Any short fragrance refuses the whole with 409 `oil_short` naming it; a set that is not five is 422. Audited `warehouse.set_bottled`. It works for either set; the manual on-hand count stays as the correction.

**The gift sample.** Settings in «Магазин»: `giftSample.enabled` (default on) and `giftSample.fragranceId`, picked on the settings page from the catalog by name (`picker: 'fragrance'`; 0, the default option, is the visible fragrance with the most 5 ml available, ties by name). A chosen fragrance that is gone or hidden gives no sample and says so, on the settings page, the stock page and the Home, never a silent off. The bottle is always 5 ml; the cost model's `cost.order.giftSampleMl` is its fill in the cost only, nothing duplicated. **Its stock warns:** `giftSample.lowStock` (default 10): the chosen fragrance's available 5 ml, or every visible fragrance's together while the most-stocked one is picked, at or under it shows «Пробники 5 мл заканчиваются» on the stock page and one more item in the Home's stock queue, so samples never run out unnoticed (each 5 ml row stays out of the «Мало» filter). A customer's **first order**, derived, never stored, is one placed while they have no order that is not cancelled: a cancelled first order leaves the sample for the next, a paid, delivered or returned one has had it. Checkout holds the customer's row lock, so two orders of one customer cannot both be first. The sample is picked before the stock locks, its 5 ml volume locked with the others in ascending id, and checked again under the lock: the last bottle goes to one order; the other gets none and is placed all the same. It is **an order line** (`order_lines.gift_sample`): `kind` volume on the 5 ml, unit and regular price 0, quantity 1, last in the order. So it is reserved, handed over (a `sale` stock move), given back by a cancel and returned with back-to-stock exactly like any line, and no code path needed for stock is new. On a Return the dialog lists it with the other lines: staff flag it back when the bottle came back sealed, as for any line; a returned first order has had its sample all the same. A returned 5 × 5 ml set line goes back onto its own set's shelf. It shows on the customer's order («Пробник в подарок»), the «Заказ оформлен» message, the group card (under the lines, never squeezed out), the admin order and the orders workbook. It is **not a Discount** (ADR 0002): it changes no price, total or amount due and rides with any discount; it opens no upgrade right, «Повторить заказ» leaves it out, and a review is not shown on its fragrance's card for it.

**Its cost.** The cost snapshot (#77) gives the line the calculator's `giftCost` for its own fragrance (`giftSampleCost([oil price])`: the 5 ml vial's liquid scaled to the fill, bottle, label, spray and filling, the defect %, and one QR card) as its `unit_cost`, keeps it out of the goods and puts it in the order costs' gift sample, which until now was 0. No oil price for that fragrance, no profit, as for any line. The sales report leaves the sample out of its per-fragrance and per-volume rows: its cost is already in the order costs that the paid lines share.

**The set-discount scope.** `entitlements.setDiscountScope`: `set_fragrances` (default, TZ §3.4) or `any_fragrance` (the calculator). `quote()` reads it when it prices (`rightCovers`), so a change applies to rights already open; a right from a single 10 ml bottle always covers its own fragrance only. The fragrance card's right prices and «Мои скидки» follow it (`anyFragrance`, and the card then names no five).

## Considered Options

- **A third line kind `discovery_set_5`.** Rejected: one concept, two sizes; every switch on `discovery_set` would have doubled, and the order line already carried the ml.
- **A second table for the 5 × 5 ml set.** Rejected: the same columns twice and every read written twice.
- **The 5 ml stock outside `volumes`** (a column on the fragrance). Rejected: the sample would need its own reserve, handover, cancel and return paths; as a volume line it gets them all from ADR 0010.
- **The sample as a field of the order.** Rejected for the same reason, and the group card, the workbook and the profit read lines.
- **A stored «sample given» flag on the customer.** Rejected: a cancel would have to clear it; derived from the orders it cannot drift (ADR 0005's rule for discount sources).
- **The calculator's average `giftCost` over all fragrances.** Rejected for an order: the server knows which fragrance went in, so its own oil price is exact; the planner (#78) keeps the average.
