# Stock in two levels: each fragrance's oil balance in grams, turned into bottles on hand by a bottling; one history of stock moves

The customer counts his stock as oil and perfume liquid, not only as bottles (Sardor, 2026-11-18: the warehouse from his calculator, in full; ticket #76). Until now the shop knew only **on hand** per volume (ADR 0010). It now keeps two levels per fragrance: the **oil balance** (what the supplier sold, in grams) and the bottles on hand per volume. A **bottling** moves oil from the first level into the second. Available = on hand − reserve is unchanged, and the shop still sells bottles only.

**The oil balance** is a column on the fragrance (`fragrances.oil_mg`), whole milligrams, never below zero (a check). A bottling's oil comes out in fractions of a gram (10 ml at the defaults takes 3.09 g), so grams with three decimals are kept exactly as integers. The API speaks grams.

**An oil receipt** (`oil_receipts`) is typed from the supplier's invoice: supplier, invoice number, the day it came in, grams, so'm per gram before the discount, the supplier discount % and the delivery to Termez %. The two percents start from the cost settings (ADR 0040) but belong to the invoice, so a receipt keeps its own. Its landed price is the cost model's `landedOilPrice` with those percents. A receipt adds its grams to the balance. **A correction** rewrites it, with a reason, and moves the balance by the change in grams. **A void** takes its grams back off, with a reason, and keeps the row. Both are refused (409 `oil_short`, with what is needed, what the balance holds and the shortfall) when the oil has already gone into bottles. A receipt does not change the fragrance's oil price, the cost model's input: the owner updates that in the fragrance form (open question).

**Draft receipts** come from the purchase planner (#78): «Черновики приходов по плану» on `/purchase` sends the plan's grams per fragrance, and the server makes a draft per fragrance at its oil price, with the settings' discount and import %, dated today, the supplier from the fragrance and no invoice number. A draft holds no oil and leaves no stock move: it is not stock yet, only an intention. The owner **confirms** it on the fragrance's page as the invoice reads (supplier, number, date and grams required), and only then does its oil join the balance, as a new receipt would (audited as `warehouse.oil_received` with `fromDraft`). An unwanted draft is **discarded** (deleted, audited); a draft is never corrected or voided, and a confirmed receipt is never discarded. The stock page counts a fragrance's drafts. A draft is a flag on the receipt (`oil_receipts.draft`) rather than its own table, so confirming it keeps its id and its fields.

**A bottling** takes counts per volume. The oil it uses is the cost model's `liquidNeeded` for each volume times its count, filling loss included, summed unrounded and rounded once to the milligram, at the liquid settings of that moment (they go into the audit entry). It is refused (409 `oil_short`) when the balance holds less. Otherwise, in one transaction, the balance falls and each volume's on hand rises. 5 ml and the 5 × 5 ml set come with #79: a bottling accepts any volume the fragrance has, and refuses (422) one it does not.

**Ethanol is costed, not stocked.** It is one bulk liquid for every fragrance, bought by the litre, already a cost setting, and the calculator keeps no stock of it. A bottling records the ethanol it took (`bottlings.ethanol_ml`) and the audit says it; nothing counts it down. If the owner wants an ethanol balance, it becomes a third stock beside the oil, without a per-fragrance level.

**Litres.** The oil balance reads as the litres of perfume it still makes: `liquidFromOil`, the same formula read backwards (grams ÷ density ÷ oil % ÷ (1 + loss)). So «0.32 l» is what a bottling can still use. The calculator's planner (`buyCalc`) takes the loss off as × (1 − loss) and multiplies by the density instead of dividing. At density 1 the two differ by about 0.1 % (3.236 against 3.233 litres from a kilo); the planner (#78) keeps the calculator's own figures. The **low-oil warning** is a setting in litres of perfume (`stock.lowOilLitres`, default 1): a fragrance at or under it is «Мало масла», with none «Нет масла»; 0 warns only when nothing is left.

**Stock value at cost** (owner only): the oil balance at the average landed price of the fragrance's standing receipts (each weighted by its grams), plus the bottles on hand at their unit cost (ADR 0040, from the fragrance's oil price). Without an oil price the bottles have no value, so neither does the total. A moving average or FIFO was rejected: corrections and voids would have to rewrite it backwards, and the owner checks against invoices, not lots.

**The history** is one append-only table, `stock_moves`: one row per change of a fragrance's oil balance or bottles on hand, written by the transaction that made it, with signed changes (oil in mg, bottles by ml), the actor, a reason, and what the line needs to read (the invoice, the order, where a count came from). Its kinds are `oil_receipt`, `oil_receipt_corrected`, `oil_receipt_voided`, `bottling`, `sale` (the BTS handover), `returned` (a parcel back or a return to stock, in the order transition) and `correction` (a count from the stock page, the fragrance form or the Excel import); an oil receipt is never called a bare «receipt», which is the shopper's payment proof (GLOSSARY). The seed writes none. The history starts when this ships; counts before it are not replayed. **The on-hand edit stays as a manual correction and now needs a reason** (`PATCH /admin/volumes/:id/on-hand`, and the Discovery set's, whose reason goes to the audit since the set has no fragrance history).

**Locks.** An oil receipt, its correction or void, and a bottling lock the fragrance row first (`FOR NO KEY UPDATE`), then a bottling locks the volumes in ascending id, as checkout and the fragrance form do. Two bottlings of one fragrance queue on its row, so the second sees what the first left. `stock_moves.fragrance_id` has no foreign key on purpose. The fragrance form and the catalog import take `FOR UPDATE` on the fragrance and then lock its volumes; the BTS handover holds volume locks and then writes a move. A key check on the fragrance would wait behind the form's `FOR UPDATE` (a key share lock conflicts with it, not with a bottling's `NO KEY UPDATE`) while the form waits for the handover's volumes: a deadlock. Fragrances are never deleted, so the warehouse tables' other keys `restrict` a delete instead of cascading.

**Roles** (ADR 0017: managers handle stock). Owner and manager: the stock page with the oil, the fragrance's warehouse page, bottling, on-hand counts, the history and the Excel export. Owner only: oil receipts (they carry purchase prices, like the oil price on the fragrance form), their invoices and suppliers, and stock values. A manager's page and file leave them out: a manager's history says oil came in and how much, not from whom or on which invoice.

**Surfaces.** `GET /admin/stock` carries each fragrance's oil and the `oilLow` filter. `GET /admin/warehouse/fragrances/:id` is the warehouse page (`/stock/:id` in the panel). `POST …/fragrances/:id/oil-receipts`, `PUT /admin/warehouse/oil-receipts/:id`, `POST …/oil-receipts/:id/void`, `POST …/fragrances/:id/bottlings`, and for drafts `POST /admin/warehouse/oil-receipt-drafts`, `POST …/oil-receipts/:id/confirm`, `DELETE /admin/warehouse/oil-receipts/:id`. `GET /admin/warehouse/export?lang=` is the workbook: the stock sheet and the whole history. Audit actions `warehouse.oil_received`, `warehouse.oil_receipt_corrected`, `warehouse.oil_receipt_voided`, `warehouse.bottled`, `warehouse.exported`, `warehouse.oil_receipt_drafts_created`, `warehouse.oil_receipt_draft_discarded`.

## Considered Options

- **The oil balance as the sum of the history.** Rejected: the stored column carries a check that it never goes below zero, and the history can still be summed against it (the int test does).
- **Oil in grams as `numeric`.** Rejected: whole milligrams in `integer` add up exactly with no string parsing, and the balance stays under 2 tonnes.
- **History composed at read time** from the receipts, bottlings, order events and audit entries. Rejected: four sources with four shapes, and the audit's payloads are not a contract.
- **Stock ethanol too.** Not now: see above.
- **The bottling's oil in its own formula** (bottles × ml × oil %). Rejected: the cost model's `liquidNeeded` is the one formula (ADR 0040), loss and density included.
