# Stock in liquid: each fragrance's ready concentrate and oil, bottles filled at handover, consumables counted on their own

The customer (2026-11-20, videos 2 and 3 on `/stock`; ticket #82): «5, 10, 30, 50 ml liklarni umuman olib tashlashimiz kerak … gotovaya konsentratsiya … millilitr hisobida, undan keyin parfum masla … Boshqa narsa kerak emas, chunki tayyor bo'ladi mana shu ikkalasidan.» He keeps perfume as liquid and fills a bottle when it ships, so a count of bottles per volume is a number nobody can check on the shelf. This supersedes the bottle level of ADR 0041 (bottles on hand per volume, bottling) and the bottle stock of ADR 0010 and ADR 0042. What those ADRs say about the oil balance, oil receipts and their drafts, the history, the reserve's orders, the Discovery sets, the gift sample and the roles stands.

**Two levels per fragrance, both read in ml.**

- **Ready concentrate** («Готовая концентрация» / «Tayyor konsentrat»): the perfume liquid, mixed and ready to pour. `fragrances.concentrate_ul`, whole microlitres, `bigint` (a tonne of oil makes more than int32 µl), never below zero (a check). The API speaks ml with three decimals.
- **Oil** («Парфюм масло» / «Atir moyi»): the existing oil balance, `fragrances.oil_mg`. Receipts stay in grams as invoices read; the panel shows the oil in ml as well, grams ÷ the `cost.liquid.oilDensity` setting.

**No bottle stock.** `volumes.on_hand` is dropped: a volume keeps its price, markdown and photos, and nothing counts bottles any more. The **fill** of a bottle is the cost model's own liquid with the filling loss, `fillUl(ml)` = round(ml × (1 + `cost.liquid.fillingLossPercent` / 100) × 1000) µl in `apps/api/src/cost/unit-cost.ts` (ADR 0040's `filled`, one formula), at the settings of the moment. Every count of liquid is a whole number of µl, so a reserve, a handover and a return add up exactly.

**Reserve and available.** The reserve keeps ADR 0010's orders (`RESERVING_STATUSES`) and lines (`lineVolumeUnits`: a volume line, the Gift sample and a Promo gift are their quantity of their volume; a builder set line is one 10 ml of each of its five), now counted in liquid per fragrance: Σ quantity × `fillUl(ml)` (`reservedLiquid`). **Available bottles** of a volume = ⌊(concentrate − reserve) ÷ `fillUl(ml)`⌋, never below zero, and 0 for a hidden fragrance in the shop as before. The volumes of a fragrance share one liquid, so a cart takes them in line order from the fragrance's free liquid (`load-cart.ts`), and the last ml can't be sold twice. `available`, `belowReserve` and `toVolumeCard` keep their one place in `catalog.service.ts`, fed by the fragrance's liquid instead of a volume's count. The reserve is read at the settings of now, so a new filling loss moves it; the handover takes what the reserve held.

**Locks.** One order for every path that touches stock: the **fragrance rows in ascending id** (`FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, the warehouse's lock), then the **Discovery set rows** in ascending vial ml (`FOR UPDATE`), then the **consumables** in ascending id. Checkout, the BTS handover, a cancel or return, an Uzum sale or its void, mixing, set assembly and every count follow it. Checkout locks the fragrances of every line (the five of a builder set, a Promo gift's, the Gift sample's), so a concurrent checkout waits and then sees this order's reserve. The fragrance form and the catalog import keep `FOR UPDATE` on the fragrance; nothing locks a volume for stock any more, so ADR 0041's volume-then-fragrance deadlock is gone. `stock_moves.fragrance_id` still has no foreign key.

**Handover and return.** «Передан в BTS» takes each line's liquid off its fragrance's concentrate (Σ quantity × fill) and a Discovery set line's boxes off its set's count. «Не забрал в BTS» (a cancel after handover) and a return's back-to-stock lines put the same back. Neither is ever refused: a count that would go below zero stops at zero and the move records the shortfall («Пересчитайте» on the row). An **Uzum sale** takes its liquid and boxes at once, within available (409 `stock_short` otherwise, ADR 0041's rule), and its **void** puts them back. The **Gift sample** is a 5 ml fill from concentrate: checkout writes the order's lines first and then checks, under the locks and with those lines reserved, that a 5 ml fill is still free, so the sample never takes liquid the order's own bottles need.

**Mixing replaces bottling.** «Приготовить концентрат» / «Konsentrat tayyorlash» (owner and manager): the ml of concentrate made. It takes the oil and the ethanol in that ml at the liquid settings of now, `mixNeeds(ml)`: oil grams = ml × `oilPercent` / 100 × density, ethanol ml = ml × `ethanolPercent` / 100, **without the filling loss** — the loss happens when a bottle is filled, and `fillUl` already counts it. `liquidNeeded` (a bottle's liquid with the loss) is `mixNeeds(filled(ml))`, so the two cannot drift. Oil is rounded once to the milligram, ethanol to the whole ml (half up). In one transaction: the fragrance, then the ethanol consumable; refused with 409 `oil_short` (what it needs, what the balance holds, the shortfall in grams) or `ethanol_short` (the same in ml; no active ethanol item counts as none) and nothing written; otherwise oil and ethanol fall and the concentrate rises by exactly the ml asked. One stock move (`mixing`) holds all three changes and the settings it used; audited `warehouse.mixed`. No table of its own: the move is the record. Old bottlings and their moves stay in the history as they were.

**Discovery sets stay ready boxes with their own count** (ADR 0009, 0042). **Set assembly** («Собрать наборы» / «Toʻplam yigʻish», `POST /admin/warehouse/sets/:vialMl/assemblies`, owner and manager) replaces the set bottling: N boxes take one fill of the set's vial ml from each of the five fragrances' concentrate, within what is available (the reserve stays for the orders that hold it; 409 `concentrate_short` naming the fragrance), and one flacon of the vial ml per vial. Five fragrances (ascending id), the set row, the flacon; a `set_assembly` move per fragrance; audited `warehouse.set_assembled`. The manual count with a reason stays the correction.

**Consumables** («Расходники» / «Sarf materiallari»), the third stock, counted on their own: table `consumables` with RU/UZ name, unit (`pcs` or `ml`), on hand (whole units), a low threshold, an optional link (a **use**: `flacon` or `box` with its ml, `ethanol`, or `other` for none) and an archived flag. One active item per link (a partial unique index). Seeded by the migration: «Флакон 5 / 10 / 30 / 50 мл», «Коробка 10 / 30 / 50 мл» and «Спирт» (ml). Staff add items (the customer: «joy qoldirib ketishimiz kerak»), rename, archive, **receive** (+, with an optional note) and **correct** a count (compare-and-set against the count shown, with a reason). **Linked use:** a bottle filled at handover or on Uzum takes one flacon of its ml and, sold alone at 10 / 30 / 50 ml (a Promo gift too), one box of its ml; a builder set's five vials take a 10 ml flacon each and no box (a set's vials have none, ADR 0040), the Gift sample a 5 ml flacon and no box; a set assembly takes its flacons; mixing takes ethanol. A return or void gives the same back. **Consumables never gate a sale:** a shortfall at handover, on Uzum or at assembly stops the count at zero, records the shortfall on the move and flags the item `needs_recount` («Пересчитайте» / «Qayta sanang») until a correction clears it. Only mixing is refused for short ethanol (the ticket's rule). Their level is the item's own threshold. Integer units: ethanol is poured by eye far coarser than half a ml.

**The history** stays one table (`stock_moves`, ADR 0041) with kinds added rather than a second history: `mixing`, `set_assembly`, `consumable_received`, `consumable_corrected`. A move now carries a signed `concentrate_ul` and `consumables` (`{ id: change }`) beside the oil and `bottles` (which now say what was filled, handed over or returned); its `fragrance_id` is null for a consumable-only move (a check: a fragrance or a consumable change). A handover's flacons and boxes ride on the move of the fragrance whose bottle they hold, so a consumable's history is the moves that name it (`consumables ? id`). A shortfall goes into `details.short` (`concentrateUl`, `consumables`). A **concentrate count** from the stock page, the fragrance form («Концентрат, мл») or the catalog import is a `correction` with its source, compare-and-set like the old on-hand edit, with a reason on the stock page; audited `warehouse.concentrate_corrected`.

**Thresholds** (settings): `stock.lowConcentrateMl` (ml of available concentrate, default 300): at or under it «Мало концентрата», and «Нет концентрата» while it can't fill a 10 ml bottle. `stock.lowOilMl` (ml of oil, default 300) replaces `stock.lowOilLitres` (litres of perfume the oil makes; the migration drops its stored value): the oil is read in ml now. `stock.lowAvailableThreshold` keeps the Discovery sets' boxes; `giftSample.lowStock` keeps the 5 ml samples (now 5 ml fills of free concentrate).

**Stock value at cost** (owner only): the oil at the average landed price of its standing receipts (ADR 0041), plus the concentrate at the cost model's liquid per ml (the fragrance's oil at its landed oil price, the ethanol and the fixative, no loss: `concentrateCost`); without an oil price the concentrate has no value, so neither has the total. Consumables have no prices yet.

**Migration** (`0043`), hand-written data steps as 0040 did: each fragrance's bottles on hand fold into concentrate (on hand × `fillUl(ml)` at the stored filling loss, else its default) and into the flacon and box counts (a filled bottle holds a flacon and, at 10 / 30 / 50 ml, a box); one `correction` move per fragrance with the source `migration` records it (bottles −, concentrate +, flacons and boxes +), so the history adds up from day one. Then `volumes.on_hand` goes. The seed's placeholder stock is concentrate (`PLACEHOLDER_CONCENTRATE_ML`, ours like the placeholder prices its volumes stay flagged for), in the seed workbook's «Концентрат, мл» column on «Ароматы»; its volumes sheet has no count.

**Surfaces.** `GET /admin/stock`: per fragrance concentrate, reserve, available, the bottles each sold volume still makes, oil (g and ml), tags and the value; the Discovery sets (page 1). The consumables are their own list beside it on the page (`GET /admin/consumables`). `PUT /admin/warehouse/fragrances/:id/concentrate`, `POST …/fragrances/:id/mixings`, `POST /admin/warehouse/sets/:vialMl/assemblies`, `GET|POST /admin/consumables`, `PUT /admin/consumables/:id`, `POST …/:id/receipts`, `POST …/:id/counts`, `GET …/:id/history`. `PATCH /admin/volumes/:id/on-hand`, the bottling and the set bottling routes are gone. The warehouse workbook: stock, consumables and the whole history. The Uzum picker's «доступно N» is available bottles. Roles as ADR 0041: owner and manager count, mix, assemble and handle consumables; values and oil receipts are the owner's.

## Considered Options

- **Keep bottles per volume and add the concentrate beside them.** Rejected: the customer said to remove them; two counts of one liquid would drift.
- **Available as each volume's own share of the concentrate** (a split by the planner's shares). Rejected: the shelf holds one liquid; any volume may take it.
- **The reserve frozen in µl on each order line** at checkout. Rejected for now: the reserve stays derived (ADR 0010) and needs no backfill; a changed filling loss moves it, which the owner sees on the stock page.
- **Consumables gate sales** (no flacon, no sale). Rejected by the ticket: the count is what is wrong, not the parcel.
- **A second history table for consumables.** Rejected: one history, new kinds (ADR 0041's reasons).
- **Concentrate in 0.01 ml.** Rejected: µl keeps the fill's rounding below a drop and the arithmetic in integers.
